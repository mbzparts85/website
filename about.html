COMPANY PROFILE

MBZ TRADING – Auto Spare Parts Importer

MBZ TRADING is an international auto spare parts importing and wholesale trading company specializing in sourcing and supplying automotive components to businesses worldwide. We focus on quality products, competitive pricing and reliable sourcing solutions to meet the diverse requirements of the automotive spare parts industry.

Our Business

We import a comprehensive range of automotive spare parts from established international markets, including the United Arab Emirates (UAE), Thailand, Indonesia and China. Through our international sourcing network, we aim to provide business partners with dependable supply solutions and access to a wide variety of automotive components.

Our Product Range

  • Body Parts: Bumpers, bonnets, fenders, doors and other exterior components.
  • Electrical Parts: Sensors, switches, lighting components, wiring-related parts and electrical assemblies.
  • Suspension Parts: Shock absorbers, control arms, bushes, ball joints and related components.
  • Engine Parts: Engine components, cooling system parts, gaskets, belts and other essential mechanical parts.

Parts on Demand & Pre-Orders

We specialize in demand-based sourcing and pre-order supplies, helping customers obtain specific automotive parts according to their requirements. Whether you need particular part numbers, specialized components or bulk quantities, our team works to identify suitable sourcing options through our international supplier network.

B2B Wholesale & Bulk Supply

MBZ TRADING operates exclusively on a Business-to-Business (B2B) basis, serving automotive spare parts wholesalers, distributors, retailers, importers and other commercial buyers. We focus on wholesale and bulk orders rather than individual retail sales, supporting businesses seeking consistent supply, competitive commercial pricing and long-term trading relationships.

OUR COMMITMENT

At MBZ TRADING, we believe in building long-term business relationships through professional service, competitive pricing, transparent communication and a customer-focused approach. We strive to make automotive spare parts sourcing and supply more dependable and efficient for our business partners.
